## Abstract

Megatron is "the" training framework for LLMs. However, its code is intertwined, buggy, and not the fastest. What caused this? How can we build a minimalist training framework for 1T+ params and 1M context, with higher accuracy, better maintainability, and faster speed? Are system design and software engineering still relevant while we pray to the omnipotent AIs?

## Speaker Bio

Lequn is a Member of Technical Staff at Perplexity AI. He works on inference systems and more recently on RL training infra. He graduated from syslab 2.5 years ago.
